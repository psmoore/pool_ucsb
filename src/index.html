Aquatics & Safety

Make a splash at the UCSB Recreation Center pools: two newly plastered outdoor pools and a spa. The Olympic-size Large Pool has an attached dive well, and the Small Pool is a 25-yard, 9-lane, all-shallow teaching pool. Programs for youth and adults include SCUBA, swim lessons, log rolling, certification classes and summer camps. Pool use requires a Recreation Center Membership.

Policies

No diapers or swim diapers in the pools. If your child is not fully potty-trained, please do not bring them into the pools.

One breath, one length, one time (25 yards or less). No prolonged breath-holding, hyperventilating, repeated breath-holding or hypoxic training.
