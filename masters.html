Gaucho Masters Swim

Instructor: Staff · Quarterly and monthly passes available.

Gaucho Masters Swim helps swimmers at every level, from novices to highly competitive swimmers, triathletes and open water swimmers. Structured workouts improve efficiency, stroke technique and endurance. This is not a learn-to-swim program: you should have prior formal swimming experience.

Workouts this week

Live

Monday, Wednesday and Friday at noon, in short-course lanes 10–20 of the Large Pool. Subscribe to add the workouts to your own calendar.
